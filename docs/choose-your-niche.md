# Choose your niche — decision framework

The repo's default recommendation is **small-space home living / kos-kosan hacks**. This doc explains *why*, so you (or a stranger) can re-decide with eyes open instead of copying blindly.

## The 5 criteria (all must pass)

1. **Faceless-native** — can every post be text, a room photo, or hands-only video? Anything needing a face, skin, or body fails a faceless operation.
2. **Impulse price band** — are matching Shopee products Rp20–150rb? Above that, clicks don't convert on impulse; below Rp20rb, commission rounds to dust.
3. **Shopee-mapped pains** — does the audience already search Shopee with obvious keywords (e.g. "rak dapur susun")? If you can't name 10 search keywords in 2 minutes, the niche is wrong.
4. **Carousel-friendly** — can a winner become "5 barang under 50rb" slides and hands-only clips? Text-only niches die at Phase 4.
5. **Low claim risk** — can you write 30 posts without claiming you tested something, cured something, or whitening anything? Regulated claims (skincare efficacy, supplements, medicine) are a policy minefield.

## Three candidates scored

| Criterion | Home living / kos (✅ pick) | Beauty / skincare (lane #2) | Gadgets / tech |
|---|---|---|---|
| Faceless-native | Yes — rooms, hands, lists | No — trust lives in faces/skin | Partial — unboxings want hands + voice |
| Impulse band | Yes, dense | Yes, but premium skew | Mixed — many items >Rp500rb |
| Shopee keywords | Endless (storage, dapur, kabel) | Endless but hyper-competitive | Narrower, spec-heavy |
| Carousel-friendly | Excellent (before/after, checklists) | Excellent, but needs face proof | Weak (specs don't carousel well) |
| Claim risk | Low | **High** (BPOM, efficacy, skin-type claims) | Low, but low buying impulse |

## Validation protocol (1 week, before committing)

1. Write down 10 audience pain points from memory. If you struggle past 6, you don't know this niche — pick another.
2. Search each pain's keyword in the Shopee affiliate portal. If fewer than 7 return 4.8★ / 10rb+ Terjual / KomisiXTRA products in the impulse band, the niche can't pay.
3. Post 10 link-free value posts across 5 days. If median views stay flat at ~0 with zero replies, the account/niche pairing is suspect — change one variable (hook family first, niche second).

## Expansion rule

One niche until you have **one proven template + 30 days of EPC data**. Only then may lane #2 (beauty) open — as a *second* account, never mixed into the first. Two niches in one account at 3–5 hrs/week produces no signal anywhere.
