# FAQ — real beginner questions, short answers

Distilled from actually setting this up. Each answer links to the doc that goes deeper.

## Money & account

**Bank review pending but dashboard says "sudah bisa komisi" — am I blocked?**
No. Earning (link → commission accrual) is live; only the *payout transfer* waits on bank + KTP/NPWP review. Post warm-up content meanwhile — by day 8 the review has usually cleared.

**Under Rp500rb goes to ShopeePay — can I split Rp2jt into 4 ShopeePay transfers?**
No. Shopee pays automatically once a week; *Shopee* picks the rail by that week's amount (<Rp500rb → ShopeePay, ≥ → bank). There is no withdraw button. Early months land on ShopeePay almost by definition — keep it KYC-verified.

**When do I see first real money?**
Honest: 2–4 months of consistent posting before the first meaningful weekly payout, at 2 posts/day and a ~0.5% hit model. This is a consistency asset, not salary. If month one pays nearly nothing, that is the model working, not failing.

## Links & tags

**Pakai Tag: Ya or Tidak?**
Always **Ya**. Tidak still earns but its clicks dissolve into an unattributable pile — your monthly review becomes guesswork. The tag changes nothing about commission or buyer experience.

**Is the tag visible to buyers?**
No. It's your private label, readable only in your own Click Report. See [glossary](glossary.md).

**Where do I find tags I made before?**
Portal → Reports → **Laporan Klik / Click Report** (or Link Saya / Kelola Link) — tags show as the "Pakai Tag" column, one row per link. Your Sheet's `links` tab is the practical registry; the portal is the cross-check.

**There's no CSV export — so how do I do the "monthly export"?**
There isn't one. Copy the five Laporan Performa columns (Klik, Pesanan, Produk Terjual, Pesanan Rp, Komisi Kotor Rp) into `performance_import` by hand, monthly. 10 minutes, not a pipeline.

## Channels & accounts

**Shopee's media form has Instagram but no Threads — can I still post on Threads?**
Yes. That dropdown is identity verification, not a posting permit. Attribution rides on the tagged link, not the form. Post on Threads; if an "Other/website" slot exists, paste your Threads URL there.

**Main account or fresh faceless accounts?**
Register with whatever proves you're real (main is fine); *post* from fresh niche-faceless accounts. Never mix: one niche per account.

**Why 7 days link-free warm-up? Can't I post links now?**
New accounts that drop links week one get throttled — your tags get spent on suppressed posts. The week isn't idle: it runs in parallel with bank review and product shortlisting.

## Tools

**Buffer via the thread's `s.id` link, the Tally skill form, the ChatGPT-Go promo?**
Buffer yes, but sign up directly at buffer.com — the `s.id` link is someone's referral and your rule is own-accounts-only. Tally form: skip, P4 covers it with no data handed over. Go promo: redundant, you already hold full ChatGPT. (Plan §3.)

**Later / Hootsuite / Metricool?**
Later and Hootsuite have no free plan (trial only) — excluded. Metricool free exists but caps at 20 posts/month: analytics fallback only. Verdict table: plan §4, evidence: research §2.

## Performance

**0 clicks after 2 weeks?**
Links never seen = reach problem, not product problem. Check views first, then comment timing (≤60 min), then topic-to-product match. Full tree: plan Appendix C.

**Clicks but Rp0 commission?**
Normal early: commission counts only completed + validated orders; the dashboard shows gross estimates first. Judge after one full weekly payout cycle, not before.

**When do I quit a template / the niche?**
Template: 30 days of data, bottom-quartile views + EPC → STOP, reallocate. Niche: 150+ posts with zero >3x-median outliers → change one variable (hook family first, niche second). Never hop weekly.
